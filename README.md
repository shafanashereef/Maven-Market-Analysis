# Maven Market Analysis

## Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **business performance and analytics assessment** of the *Maven Market* dataset, covering the period **1997–1998**. The analysis focuses on understanding overall performance, identifying growth drivers and risks, and translating insights into **actionable, executive-level recommendations**.

The work is designed for **business stakeholders and decision-makers**, emphasizing clarity, storytelling, and strategic relevance rather than raw data exploration.

---

## Objectives

The primary objectives of this analysis are to:

* Evaluate overall business performance across **Revenue, Profit, Sales, Customers, and Products**
* Identify key **growth drivers**, operational strengths, and risk areas
* Understand **customer behavior and segmentation**
* Assess **product and brand profitability**, including the impact of costs and returns
* Provide **clear, actionable recommendations** to support future growth

---

## Scope of Analysis

The analysis is structured around four core dashboards:

1. **Executive Summary** – Overall business health and performance trends
2. **Sales Performance Analysis** – Transaction behavior, store execution, and sales momentum
3. **Customer Analytics** – Customer demographics, value drivers, and geographic distribution
4. **Product Performance & Profitability** – Revenue vs cost, returns, margins, and optimization opportunities

---

## Dataset Overview

* **Time Period:** 1997–1998
* **Total Customers:** ~10K
* **Total Transactions:** ~270K
* **Total Revenue:** $1.76M
* **Total Profit:** $1.05M

### Key Dimensions

* Time (Daily, Monthly, YTD)
* Products and Brands
* Store Type and Geography
* Customer Demographics (Age, Income, Occupation, Household)
* Returns, Cost, and Price Adjustments

## Key Insights

### Business Performance

* The business demonstrates **strong financial health**, with consistently **high margins (~60%)**
* Revenue growth shows a **clear inflection in early 1998**, followed by predictable, execution-led growth
* Profit growth is **volume-driven**, not margin-driven
* Large-format stores (**Supermarket and Deluxe**) drive scale without margin dilution

### Sales Performance

* Transaction growth becomes **steady and predictable**, especially in 1998
* Revenue and demand are **well distributed across products**, reducing dependency risk
* **Store execution matters more than city size**, with Salem and Tacoma outperforming peers
* **Thursdays are peak transaction days**, presenting optimization opportunities

### Customer Analytics

* Highest-value customers are **middle-to-upper income ($30K–$90K)**, **married homeowners**
* Customer base skews **older**, with repeat purchasing driving stability
* **Professionals and skilled manual workers** generate the majority of revenue
* Customer concentration is strongest on the **West Coast**, particularly California
* Gender distribution is balanced; **life stage and household profile** matter more than gender

### Product Performance & Profitability

* **High revenue does not always equal high profit**; costs and returns drive net performance
* Profit margins are consistently strong, limiting differentiation through pricing alone
* **Returns and cost control** are the primary levers for profit improvement
* Some high-selling products suffer from **structural cost inefficiencies**, not demand issues

---

## Executive Summary Insight

> **The business is a scalable, high-margin, volume-driven operation. The next phase of growth will come from operational efficiency, disciplined execution, and portfolio optimization—not from margin expansion.**

---

## Recommendations

* Scale volume through **large-format stores** and replicate best-performing execution models
* Prioritize **cost and returns reduction** in high-revenue, low-profit products
* Focus growth investments on **core customer segments** with strong repeat behavior
* Use **execution-based performance metrics** rather than geography size alone
* Simplify and optimize the **product portfolio**, fixing or exiting inefficient SKUs
* Pursue **selective geographic expansion** beyond the West Coast with disciplined pilots
